N·01 / Capital资本2026.11.18

Capacity comes with a calendar.算力也有付款日。

The prospectus describes take-or-pay compute contracts; some pass fuel costs through. Capital spending can be substantial before the capacity earns revenue.招股书披露，部分算力合同用不足也须付款，有些还将燃料成本转嫁给客户。产能产生收入之前，资本开支就可能相当可观。

Watch关注点 — Compare payment schedules with revenue from the capacity they buy.对照付款时间表与相应产能开始创收的时间。

N·02 / Legal法律2026.11.18

A charge belongs to its year.和解费用要看入账年份。

The filing looks back at Bartz: Anthropic booked the $1.5 billion copyright settlement charge in 2025. District court approval followed in July 2026.招股书回顾了 Bartz 版权案：Anthropic 在 2025 年计入 15 亿美元和解费用，地区法院于 2026 年 7 月批准和解。

Watch关注点 — Keep this historical cost separate from recurring expenses; the filing is not a new ruling.区分历史费用与经常性支出；此次披露并非新的裁决。

N·03 / Markets市场2026.11.16

Two deposit days, one narrow exception.两次存款日重叠，一项有限例外。

In its SIFMA letter, SEC staff offered limited no-action relief on broker-dealer reserve treatment for overlapping deposit days in the Anthropic IPO.在给 SIFMA 的函件中，SEC 工作人员针对 Anthropic 上市期间存款日重叠的情况，就经纪交易商准备金处理给予有限的不采取执法行动安排。

Watch关注点 — Read the qualifying dates and transactions, not a signal of system-wide stress.看适用的日期和交易范围，不把特例当作系统性压力的信号。

N·05 / Power · background电力 · 背景2026

A grid forecast, not an AI meter.电网预测，不是 AI 电表。

The IEA puts global data centre electricity use at 485 TWh in 2025. Its updated central projection reaches 950 TWh in 2030, covering all data centres, not just AI.国际能源署给出的 2025 年全球数据中心用电量为 485 太瓦时。其更新后的中心情景预计 2030 年达 950 太瓦时，范围涵盖所有数据中心，不只计算 AI。

Watch关注点 — Compare future measured demand with the projection, not with a presumed AI total.将未来的实际用电与预测对照，不把预测当作 AI 用电总量。

02  /  Opinion · ANT short thesis观点 · ANT 做空论点

The cost of waiting.等待的代价。

Our bear case is about timing, not insolvency. If customers fill new capacity slowly, the bill for reserved compute could arrive well before the revenue to cover it.我们的看空论点在于时间差，而非资不抵债。若客户消化新增产能缓慢，预留算力的账单可能远早于足以覆盖它的收入到来。

The contrary case反方论据

Fresh funding buys time. If demand absorbs capacity on schedule, reserving compute early could be an advantage rather than a burden.新资金延长了缓冲期。如果需求按期消化产能，提前锁定算力可能成为优势，而非负担。

What would change our mind什么会推翻论点

Sustained high utilization alongside revenue that covers contracted costs would undermine the timing thesis. A share-price move alone would not test it.若利用率持续高企，且收入足以覆盖合同成本，时间差论点就难以成立。单凭股价波动无法检验它。
