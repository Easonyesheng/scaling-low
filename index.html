01  /  Field guide阅读指南

A map of AI riskAI 风险图谱

A / Misuse滥用

Who is asking?谁在使用？

A convincing voice can help someone communicate or help an impostor borrow their identity. Before acting on an urgent request, confirm it through a familiar channel.逼真的合成语音可以帮助人们表达，也可能被用来冒充他人。遇到紧急请求，不妨先通过熟悉的渠道向本人核实。

B / Unreliable outputs输出失准

Does the citation exist?引文有出处吗？

A fluent answer can name a study that was never published. In a clinic or a classroom, someone needs time to check the original before relying on the answer.流畅的回答也可能引用一项从未发表的研究。在诊室或课堂里，把答案用作依据之前，需要留出核对原文的时间。

C / Systemic impact系统性影响

Whose grid is it?谁在共用电网？

A data centre plugs into the same grid as its neighbours. Its annual energy use tells only part of the story: planners also need to know how much power it draws at the busiest hour.数据中心与周边居民共用电网。年耗电量只能说明一部分问题：规划者还得知道，在用电最紧张的时段，它需要多大功率。

04  /  Opinion · A small manifesto观点 · 一份小宣言

Give enough a benchmark.给「够用」定个标准。

Start with the job, not the model. If a small tool gets a reader to the right document, a larger one should earn its place: does it find what the first one missed, and can the reader check?先问要做什么，再选模型。如果小工具已能帮读者找到所需文献，更大的模型就得说明自己的用处：它找到了哪些遗漏？读者能核实吗？
