Countdown method倒计时怎么算？

Target = round((technical UTC timestamp × technical weight + economic UTC timestamp × economic weight) ÷ 100), in milliseconds. The clock rounds the remaining time up to whole seconds. Only the display changes with your time zone.综合时点＝（技术面 UTC 时间戳 × 技术权重＋经济面 UTC 时间戳 × 经济权重）÷ 100，四舍五入到毫秒。剩余时间向上取整到秒。时区只改变显示，不改变剩余时间。
